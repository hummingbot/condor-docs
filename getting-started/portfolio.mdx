Once you've [added credentials](/getting-started/credentials), you can see your balances across every connected exchange and Gateway wallet.

## In Telegram

Send `/portfolio`. Condor replies with one message for your active server:

- **Header**: the server name and a status dot (🟢 online, 🔴 offline, 🟠 authentication error).
- **💵 Total**: the value of everything you hold on that server.
- **Per account, per connector**: each connector with a balance shows its total, then a table of its tokens worth $1 or more, with columns **Token**, **Price**, **Value** and **%Tot** (share of your total). Connectors are sorted by value, largest first. Each table shows up to 10 tokens and notes how many more there are.

Below the message:

- **One button per connector.** Tap it to see that connector's full list of tokens worth $1 or more. **⬅️ Back to Overview** returns to the summary.
- **🔄 Refresh** re-fetches balances from the exchanges. The first view comes from Condor's cache, which is at most about a minute old.

If you've chosen which networks your Gateway wallets use, only those networks appear. Exchange balances are never filtered.

## In the dashboard

The dashboard's **Portfolio** page shows more than Telegram:

- Totals for your bots, executors and agents
- A value-over-time chart for 1D, 1W or 1M
- Your top holdings
- Your open positions and LP ranges

See [Portfolio](/webui/portfolio). Send `/web` in Telegram for a login link.

## Asking Condor

You can also ask about your balances in plain words, in Telegram or the dashboard chat. For example: *"What's my total balance?"*, *"How much SOL do I hold?"* or *"What positions do I have open?"* Condor looks it up with its `get_portfolio_overview` tool.
