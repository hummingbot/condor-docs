The **DEX** page is for pools Condor trades through [Gateway](/webui/settings#gateway). Find a pool, open it, then swap in it or open a concentrated-liquidity position in it.

## Your LP positions

If you have open LP positions, a **Your LP positions** strip appears above the browser. Each card shows the pair, the venue, whether the position is in range, its value, PnL and fees earned. Click a card to open its pool. The strip doesn't appear when you have no open positions.

## Browsing pools

- **Network selector**: the chain to browse. The list comes from your Gateway: only networks it's configured for that GeckoTerminal also indexes, with testnets dropped. When there's only one chain, the selector is hidden.
- **Trending**, **Top** and **New** list the chain's pools from GeckoTerminal. The **All DEXes** filter narrows them to one or more DEXes.
- **Search** takes an address only. Paste a pool address to get an **Open pool** link, or a token address to list its pools. Searching by ticker is not supported.
- **Meteora** and **Orca** (Solana only) list pools from those venues directly. These tabs add **Fee/TVL**, **APY** and **Bin** (bin step) columns, and their search box filters pools by name.
- **Favorites** lists pools you've starred. Click the star on any row to add or remove one. Favorites are saved in your browser.

Every listing shows the pair, DEX, TVL, 24h volume and 24h price change. Click a column header to sort. The last column shows what Condor can do in the pool: **LP** means you can open a liquidity position there, and **swap only** means you can only trade.

Pool data comes from GeckoTerminal, which rate-limits requests. If the limit is hit, a notice appears above the table and clears on its own.

## Inside a pool

Click a row to open the pool page.

  <img src="/images/webui/dex-pool.jpg" alt="A Meteora STONK-SOL pool page: pool stats header, candle chart with a liquidity depth column on the right, and the Order and LP tabs in the side panel" />
</Frame>

- **Header**: the pair, pool address, network, DEX, TVL, 24h volume and 24h change.
- **Interval** (`1m`, `5m`, `15m`) and **Window** (`1h` to `30d`) for the chart.
- **Position bar**: if you have an LP position in this pool, a bar above the chart shows whether it's in range, its bounds, where the price sits between them, its value, fees and PnL. Click it to highlight the position on the chart.
- **Liquidity depth**: on pools where Condor can read liquidity bins from Gateway (Solana pools such as Meteora), a column beside the chart shows where liquidity sits, on the same price scale. The range you're setting is drawn across it. Use the arrow to hide or show it.
- The **bottom pane** lists executors and positions in this pool, as on the [Trade](/webui/trade#the-bottom-pane) page.

If one of the pool's tokens isn't in Gateway's token list, a banner says its balance will read 0 and offers a button to add it.

### Order and LP tabs

The side panel has two tabs:

- **Order** swaps in the pool. It creates an order executor with direction, amount and the **Market** strategy, the only one a DEX supports. Click **Create Order**.
- **LP** opens a concentrated-liquidity position in this pool. It appears only when the pool is on a CLMM venue Condor supports. Choose the side (**Range**, **Buy** or **Sell**), set the price range (drag its lines on the chart if you like), optional auto-close limits, the amounts and **Keep Position**. Meteora pools also take a strategy. Click **Create LP Position**.

**Create LP Position** creates an [LP Executor](/executors/lp-executor), which opens the position, tracks it and closes it on its auto-close limits.

The Workspace has no buttons to add liquidity to an open position, remove part of it, or collect fees without closing. To do that, use Telegram's **`/lp`** menu.

## Supported LP venues

You can open a position in pools on these venues (`condor/pool_data.py`):

| Venue | Chains |
|---|---|
| **Meteora** (DLMM) | Solana |
| **Orca** (Whirlpools) | Solana |
| **Raydium CLMM** | Solana |
| **Uniswap v3** | Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, Polygon, Optimism, BNB Chain |
| **PancakeSwap v3** | BNB Chain, Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum |

Pools on other venues, including plain AMMs and Uniswap v4, show **swap only**.

## Using Telegram

- **`/trade`** (or **`/swap`**): the DEX side of the trading menu. Pick the network and pair, tap **📋 Quote**, then **✅ Execute**.
- **`/lp`**: the liquidity menu. Browse Meteora, Orca and GeckoTerminal pools, open a position, view your positions, collect fees (one position, or **🎁 Collect All**) and close positions.
- **`/gateway`**: Gateway status, connectors, wallets, networks, pools and tokens.

## Using Hummingbot API

The Workspace creates LP positions as executors through `POST /executors/`. See [LP Executor](/executors/lp-executor#via-the-api). The API also exposes Gateway's CLMM operations directly under `/gateway/clmm/*`, including `open`, `add`, `remove`, `collect-fees`, `close` and `positions_owned`, and pool discovery at `GET /gateway/clmm/pools`. See the [API reference](/api-reference/gateway).
